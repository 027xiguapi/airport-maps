From Sheremetyevo Airport (SVO) to the city center, **shuttle bus 1195 with a change onto metro Line 2** is the easiest way in: **about an hour end to end**, for around 540 roubles.

## Why take bus 1195

The airport bus stop serves both route 851 and route 1195. **Route 1195 is the "Экспресс-автобус / Shuttle bus", which runs non-stop to Khovrino metro station (до М Ховрино / to Khovrino Metro Station)** — the northern terminus of Line 2 — and it leaves from platform C1.

## The route step by step

### 1. Clear customs and pick up your bags

After customs, head for the baggage carousel first. **Around 5 p.m. is the peak for arrivals**, when the queue is longest, so avoid that window if you are in a hurry.

![The crowd after customs](./images/1.webp)

![The baggage carousel](./images/2.webp)

### 2. Turn right and find the exit

With your bags in hand, **turn right** and follow the "Выход / Exit" signs out of the arrivals hall.

![Turning right towards the exit](./images/3.webp)

### 3. Find bus platform C1

Once outside, walk towards the **bus platforms** and look for the pillar marked **C1**. The stop sign shows a countdown to the next departure of route 1195.

![Finding platform C1](./images/4.webp)

### 4. Buy your ticket from the marshal in red and grey

The **marshal in the red-and-grey uniform** on the platform sells the tickets, so buy straight from her: **450 roubles per person, cash accepted**.

![Buying a ticket from the marshal in red and grey, 450 roubles per person](./images/5.webp)

![The ticket](./images/6.webp)

### 5. Board: the red 1195

**The red 1195** is the airport shuttle; on board there is a rack for large luggage and a screen showing the route and the stops.

![The red 1195 airport shuttle](./images/7.webp)

![The route display inside the bus](./images/8.webp)

### 6. Get off at Khovrino

The last stop is **Khovrino (Ховрино) metro station**; once you are off, just follow the crowd.

![Following the crowd from the bus to the metro station](./images/9.webp)

### 7. Buy a metro ticket and take Line 2

There are ticket machines inside the station, **90 roubles for a single ride**; 1-day, 3-day and 30-day passes are also available. The machines take **500-rouble notes**.

![The metro ticket machine: 90 roubles for a single ride](./images/10.webp)

Once through the gates, follow the signs for **Line 2** downstairs; Line 2 is the **dark green** Zamoskvoretskaya line.

![Through the ticket gates](./images/11.webp)

![Signs for Line 2 inside the station](./images/12.webp)

### 8. Ride into the city center

Line 2 runs south from Khovrino through Belorusskaya, Mayakovskaya, Tverskaya and other central stations. The author's destination this trip was **Novokuznetskaya**, leaving by exit 1.

![Navigation destination: Novokuznetskaya, exit 1](./images/13.webp)

> **Watch the station names closely**: the author rode past their stop and lost time. The gaps between stations on Line 2 are short, but the announcements are in Russian, so keep an eye on your live position on your phone.

![The lesson from riding past the stop](./images/14.webp)

You come out of the station straight into the city center.

![The station exit](./images/15.webp)

![The street outside the exit, with a busker playing on the square](./images/16.webp)

![City streets near the destination](./images/17.webp)

## Cost and time

| Item | Cost | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route 1195 airport shuttle | 450 roubles per person | about 20–30 minutes to Khovrino |
| Metro, single ride | 90 roubles | about 35 minutes to the center |
| **Total** | **about 540 roubles** | **about 1 hour** |

## Practical tips

- **You can leave your luggage on the bus**, so there is no need to hold a big case on your lap.
- **Have a 500-rouble note ready**: it works for the cash fare at the bus platform and at the metro ticket machines, and it is handier than a large note.
- The metro sells **day passes**; if you will be riding several times that day, a 1-day pass works out cheaper.
- A taxi or ride-hailing app is of course faster, but it costs several times as much and **gets stuck in traffic on the way into town at peak times**.
