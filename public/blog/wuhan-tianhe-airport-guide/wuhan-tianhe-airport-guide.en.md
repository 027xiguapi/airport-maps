With the end of the summer holidays overlapping with the return rush, Wuhan Tianhe International Airport is at its busiest. This guide covers the practical details of Terminals 2 and 3 in four parts — **domestic departures**, **domestic arrivals**, **getting to and from the airport**, and **parking**.

Tianhe has two terminals in service: **Terminal 3** (departures on level 4, arrivals on level 2) and **Terminal 2** (departures on level 2, arrivals on level 1). Check which terminal your flight uses before you set out; **Airport Route 3** shuttles between the two. The terminal plans and parking route diagrams here come from the airport's official website.

## 1. Domestic departures

### Terminal 3

1. **Arrive at the terminal.** Get there 2 hours ahead and confirm your terminal with your airline. Level 4 is the departures floor at Terminal 3.
2. **Check in and drop your bags.** Check-in islands E, F, J and K handle check-in and baggage. Check-in closes **30 minutes** before departure, so allow enough time. Self-service check-in and bag drop are available, but checking in online beforehand is a good idea.
3. **Security.** Have your boarding pass and ID or passport ready for the security officer.
4. **Wait and board.** Follow the flight information screens and signs, and be at your gate **30–40 minutes** before departure.

### Terminal 2

1. **Arrive at the terminal.** Get there 2 hours ahead and confirm your terminal with your airline. Level 2 is the departures floor at Terminal 2.
2. **Check in and drop your bags.** Check-in islands A and B handle check-in and baggage. Check-in closes **30 minutes** before departure, so allow enough time. Self-service check-in and bag drop are available, but checking in online beforehand is a good idea.
3. **Security.** Have your boarding pass and ID or passport ready for the security officer.
4. **Wait and board.** Follow the flight information screens and signs, and be at your gate **30–40 minutes** before departure.

## 2. Domestic arrivals

### Terminal 3

1. **Arrival.** If someone is meeting you, tell them in advance which terminal your flight arrives at. Level 2 is the arrivals floor at Terminal 3.
2. **Collect your baggage.** Find the carousel for your flight on the information screens. To prevent bags being taken by mistake, staff may spot-check the baggage tag against the barcode on your ticket, so keep your baggage receipt handy and cooperate.
3. **Leaving the airport.** For your own safety and security, do not accept services from anyone without official airport ID outside the restricted area, and do not take rides from unlicensed, touting drivers.

![Floor plan of Terminal 3, level 2 (arrivals)](./images/2.webp)

### Terminal 2

1. **Arrival.** If someone is meeting you, tell them in advance which terminal your flight arrives at. Level 1 is the arrivals floor at Terminal 2.
2. **Collect your baggage.** Find the carousel for your flight on the information screens, and cooperate with any spot-checks in the same way.
3. **Leaving the airport.** Do not accept services from anyone without official airport ID outside the restricted area, and do not take unlicensed rides.

## 3. Getting to and from the airport

Ground transport at Tianhe is built around the **airport buses**, **Metro Line 2**, **Airport Route 3** and the **rail station**. Every link between the two terminals, and between the terminals and the Transport Centre, is signposted.

### Airport buses

- **Hours:** 08:30 daily until the last flight; order online through the "天河畅行" (Tianhe Changxing) mini-program.
- **Frequency:** every 30 minutes, until the last flight.
- **Ticketing in person:** Terminal 2, level 1, gates 9 to 10; Terminal 3, inside gate 12.
- **Customer service:** 13387631827

| Route | Stops (fare) |
| --- | --- |
| Line 1 | T3 / T2 → Yuanboyuan North (metro, ¥10) → Minhang residential area (¥10) → Hankou Railway Station (¥12) → CBD Wealth Centre, Fanhu (¥12) → Qingnian Road (¥12) → Guqintai (¥15) → Shouyi Square (¥18) → Wuchang Railway Station (¥20) → Wushang Dream Plaza (¥20; tickets for this stop go on sale at 21:30) |
| Line 2 | T3 / T2 → Yangchahu (¥10) → Jinqiao Aeon (¥10) → Huangpu Road / Gongnongbing Road (¥12) → Xudong Shopping Mall (¥15) → Yuejiazui (¥15) → Dongting (¥18) → Chuhe Han Street (¥20) → Hongshan Square (¥20) → Wuhan Railway Station (¥45) |
| Air-rail link | Wuhan Tianhe International Airport ⇄ Wuhan Railway Station, non-stop and free of charge (provisional, 09:25–17:55); same ticketing and boarding points as the airport buses |

### Long-distance coaches

Call the customer service line for departure times and routes; counter sales are the definitive timetable. Tickets are bought at the same places as the airport buses (Terminal 2, level 1, gates 9 to 10; Terminal 3, inside gate 12), on 13387631827.

### Metro Line 2

- **Hours:** 06:00–23:00 on weekdays, 06:30–23:00 on public holidays; trains every 5–8 minutes.
- Wuhan Metro's own announcements are definitive.

Routes into and out of the airport:

- **To T3 departures:** Metro Line 2 to Tianhe Airport Station → exit B → Transport Centre level 2, link corridors 1, 2 and 3 → Terminal 3, level 4 departures hall.
- **From T3 arrivals:** Terminal 3, level 2 arrivals hall → gates 6, 7 and 8 on level 2 → metro exit B → Metro Line 2.
- **To T2 departures:** Metro Line 2 to Tianhe Airport Station → exit B → Transport Centre level 2, link corridors 1, 2 and 3 → outside gate 12 of Terminal 3 → transfer to Airport Route 3 → gate 7 of Terminal 2 → level 2 departures hall.
- **From T2 arrivals:** Terminal 2, level 1 arrivals hall → the stop at gate 10 on level 1 → transfer to Airport Route 3 → gate 12 of Terminal 3 → Transport Centre level 2, link corridors 1, 2 and 3 → metro exit B → Metro Line 2.

### Airport Route 3 (terminal shuttle)

- **Stops:** Terminal 3 gate 12 (set down and pick up) → Terminal 2, level 1, gate 7 (set down) → Terminal 2, level 1, gate 10 (pick up) → Terminal 3 gate 12 (set down and pick up).
- **Frequency:** roughly every 5 minutes.
- **Hours:** 06:00–23:00.

### Rail

Trains from Tianhe Airport Station stop at Hankou and Wuchang stations in Wuhan and at several cities across the province; the rail operator's own announcements are definitive.

- **To T3 departures:** arrive at Tianhe Airport Station → exit C → Transport Centre level 2, link corridors 1, 2 and 3 → Terminal 3, level 4 departures hall.
- **From T3 arrivals:** Terminal 3, level 2 arrivals hall → gates 6, 7 and 8 on level 2 → exit C → Tianhe Airport Station.
- **To T2 departures:** arrive at Tianhe Airport Station → metro exit B → Transport Centre level 2, link corridors 1, 2 and 3 → outside gate 12 of Terminal 3 → transfer to Airport Route 3 → gate 7 of Terminal 2 → level 2 departures hall.
- **From T2 arrivals:** Terminal 2, level 1 arrivals hall → the stop at gate 10 on level 1 → transfer to Airport Route 3 → gate 12 of Terminal 3 → Transport Centre level 2, link corridors 1, 2 and 3 → exit C → Tianhe Airport Station.

### Taxis

- **Terminal 2:** outside gate 7 on level 1; queue at the taxi rank.
- **Terminal 3:** outside gate 12 on level 1; queue at the taxi rank.

### Ride-hailing

- **Terminal 2:** level 1 arrivals hall → gate 9 on level 1 → pick up in P1 (Terminal 2 surface car park) → central Wuhan.
- **Terminal 3:** level 2 arrivals hall → gates 6, 7 and 8 on level 2 → the ride-hailing pick-up point on Transport Centre level 1 → central Wuhan.

## 4. Parking

### Terminal 3

**1. Underground car park (P7)**

S19 / S18 → Konggang Avenue → T3 underground car park → Terminal 3, level 4 departures hall (about a 5-minute walk).

549 spaces on levels B1 and B2, for small cars only.

![Signs along the route into the T3 underground car park](./images/4.webp)

**2. Transport Centre car park (P6)**

S19 / S18 → Konggang Avenue → Transport Centre car park → Transport Centre level 2, link corridors 1, 2 and 3 → Terminal 3, gates 8, 7 and 6 on level 2 → Terminal 3, level 4 departures hall (about a 10-minute walk).

3300 spaces across level B1, the ground floor and ground level 2.
