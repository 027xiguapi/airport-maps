Just back from Russia, we have put together everything we learned about the three things people ask about most: **currency exchange, bank cards and SIM cards**.

## 1. Currency exchange

### Before you leave

1. **Banks at home basically cannot sell you roubles** (in Shenzhen, at least, no bank would). The two of us carried **1800 yuan plus 600 US dollars** in cash and found it **not enough** — we would add at least another 1000 yuan on top of that.
2. If the rate at home is reasonable, change **at least 1000 roubles** in advance to cover transport out of the airport.

### At the airport

The exchange counters at the airport offer a **terrible rate, around 1 : 8.5**. So:

> **At the airport, change only enough to get into the city** — leave the large amounts for the city.

### In the city (official exchange offices)

- **GUM department store on Red Square**: there is an official exchange desk on the third floor; the rate at the time was around **1 : 11.5**.
- We did not have time to try the places with even better rates, so we make no recommendations here.

> A reminder: always use an **official exchange office** and never deal privately — you have no protection, and counterfeit notes or disputes are a real risk.

## 2. Bank cards: YooMoney (a legitimate Russian bank card)

### Registering before you leave

Sign up in advance on the **official website**. A **mobile number from your home country** is enough, and the process is quick.

### Collecting and activating the card after landing

1. **Where to collect it**: walk **out of the airport and turn left**; look for the **purple sign** — it is very obvious.
2. **Issuing**: if you have already filled in your registration details at home, showing them to the staff is enough and the card is issued on the spot.
3. **Activation**: **the three-digit code is activated inside the app** — very simple, and the staff will show you how.
4. **Topping up**: the top-up machine is right next to the desk — **choose a machine with a green ✓** (the same applies outside the airport). After topping up, a **500-rouble fee is deducted automatically**.
5. **Using it**: once activated and topped up, the card works normally.

### A few pitfalls in practice

- **Transfers by phone number often fail**: we tried several times and the money sent to a phone number never reached the other account — **transfer directly by card number instead**.
- **Watch the fees**: officially it is 3%, but in practice **a 100-rouble transfer by card number actually cost 148 roubles**. Before a real transfer, **send 2 roubles as a test**.
- **Metro**: you can tap twice for two people — no problem.
- **Buses**: after the first person taps, **tap the card again and press the button on the side** to add a passenger.

## 3. SIM cards and data

The two of us used **SIM cards bought from an online seller at home, with roaming not enabled**. What we found:

| Situation | Signal |
| --- | --- |
| Rest area at the Hermitage | No signal |
| Murmansk airport | No signal |
| Some roads in Teriberka | No signal |
| Other times and places | Normal |

> We also met other travellers whose SIM cards had no signal at all. To be safe, **prepare both a local SIM card and international roaming**: one for data, one as a backup.

## Summary

| Item | Advice |
| --- | --- |
| Cash | Carry both yuan and US dollars — more flexible than a single currency |

| Bank card | Register with YooMoney online in advance; collect it at the purple sign in the airport |
