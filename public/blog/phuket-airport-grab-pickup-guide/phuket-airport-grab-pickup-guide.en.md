Flying into Phuket from Shanghai on an international flight, the simplest way to a hotel is exactly one route: **leave through gate 8**, walk straight out, and you are at the Grab pick-up point.

## Why gate 8

Phuket's international arrivals exits are numbered. In practice, **gate 2 is confusing and hard to connect with the Grab pick-up**, whereas **gate 8 leads straight down a single path** with no fuss. So do not deliberate — head for gate 8.

## Step by step

### 1. Buy a Thai SIM when you land

Sort out a local SIM before leaving the terminal — you need data for the ride-hailing app and maps.

### 2. Clear immigration

Join the queue as usual. **Photography is not allowed in the immigration area**, so put your phone away.

### 3. Follow the signs to gate 8

Leave the terminal following the Exit signs, aiming for **exit 8**. The signs are brightly lit at night and hard to miss.

### 4. Out of gate 8, then straight ahead

Outside is a covered walkway with signs for **Public Taxi, Public Bus and Ride App**. Follow it straight along.

![The covered walkway with taxi and ride-app signs](./images/1.webp)

### 5. Find the green Grab kiosk

At the end of the walk you reach the green **Grab Pick-Up Point** sign and kiosk, with an arrow pointing to the pick-up area.

![The green Grab Pick-Up Point sign](./images/2.webp)

![The green Grab kiosk](./images/3.webp)

![The Grab kiosk with cars waiting](./images/4.webp)

### 6. Book on Grab

Open Grab, register, **link Alipay** and enter your destination. Set the pick-up point to the Grab Pick-Up Point in front of you.

![The navigation screen inside the car](./images/5.webp)

## A few tips

- **Landing at midnight** is no problem: the walkway is lit and the pick-up point is staffed.
- Phuket Airport to Patong, Kata and the other beaches is a long drive — **check the plate and model shown in the app before you get in**.
- Grab fixes the price when you book, so there is no haggling. If a driver demands more, report it in the app.
