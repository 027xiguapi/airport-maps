Golden Week is coming,

and long-haul travel is warming up.

With **visa-free entry to Türkiye for Chinese citizens** in place,

and **direct flights between China and Türkiye** getting easier every season,

Türkiye has become one of the most popular autumn destinations.

As a super-hub spanning Europe and Asia,

iGA Istanbul Airport keeps working on the passenger experience,

making every stop thoughtful, easy and comfortable.

Whether you are on a short connection, a long wait between flights,

or arriving in Istanbul to start a Turkish trip,

this practical airport guide

walks you through the Chinese-language services, lounges, family-friendly facilities and travel conveniences inside the terminal.

## 01 Travelling in Chinese, right through the airport

Travelling across borders, the last thing you want is a language barrier or a sign you cannot read. At Istanbul Airport, Chinese-speaking travellers can move through the whole journey with confidence.

### Chinese signage at every step

From the customs channels, the tax refund area and the information desk to the gates, transfer corridors and flight information screens, key points across the terminal carry **Chinese signage**, so information is immediate and easy to follow.

No struggling with translation and no wandering — look up and you will find your way. Transfers, tax refunds and boarding are clear at a glance.

![Chinese-language signage at Istanbul Airport](./images/3.webp)

### Smart video kiosks

**（Digital Kiosk）**

The terminal has **smart video assistance kiosks and self-service machines**, all supporting Chinese, where you can reach a staff member over video to ask about flights, airport facilities and more.

![Smart video kiosks](./images/5.webp)

### Chinese-speaking guides on the floor

![Chinese-speaking guides on the floor](./images/7.webp)

## 02 Comfortable ways to wait

The airport is packed with places to relax, shop, eat and rest, turning a wait into something restorative.

### TV lounges in several areas

Istanbul Airport has set up **public TV viewing areas** in several popular waiting zones, so you can catch the news or browse while you wait — no more boring layovers.

![TV viewing areas](./images/10.webp)

### Duty-free shopping and food from around the world

The terminal holds a **huge duty-free district** covering global beauty brands, luxury labels, Turkish specialities and gifts, at prices worth browsing — ideal for picking things up on the way through.

For food, **restaurants of every kind** are covered: traditional Turkish coffee, local specialities and international chains. Hot meals, light bites, desserts and drinks are all easy to find.

![Duty-free shopping and dining](./images/12.webp)

![Duty-free shopping and dining](./images/13.webp)

### iGA Lounge

On a long connection, when you want somewhere quiet to regroup, the **iGA Lounge** is a good place to land.

It is well equipped: an all-day open buffet with hot and cold drinks, showers, an outdoor viewing terrace, free high-speed WiFi and flight information displays. Inside there is also a children's play area, a prayer room, a business area and luggage lockers — so even a family travelling together can wait in comfort.

![iGA Lounge](./images/15.webp)

## 03 Family-friendly facilities

For families, the airport's facilities make departure, transit and waiting easier at every stage.

### Children's playground

The airport has a **700 m² themed children's playground**, spread across several gate and check-in areas, where children can play and burn off energy.

![Children's playground](./images/18.webp)

### Baby care

**Baby care rooms** are distributed across the whole terminal, and **strollers can be borrowed**, covering what families need at different ages.

![Baby care rooms](./images/20.webp)

![Baby care rooms](./images/21.webp)

### Family travel made easier

Dedicated **family parking spaces and family priority security lanes** cut queueing time sharply, so travelling with children runs smoothly.

![Family parking and priority lanes](./images/23.webp)

![Family parking and priority lanes](./images/24.webp)

## 04 Practical tips for travellers from China

To make Golden Week travel smoother still, here are a few more practical details:

· There are several **hot water points** in the terminal, a comfort for travellers used to drinking hot water. **Luggage trolleys** are available everywhere, so bulky bags are easy to move.

· **High-speed WiFi covers the whole terminal** — connect as soon as you land to check flights, message family and update your plans.

· Istanbul Airport **operates around the clock**, giving passengers arriving late at night, departing before dawn or on long connections more flexibility.
