![The cheapest way from Moscow's airports into the city](./images/0.webp)

Not keen on spending a few hundred on a taxi from the airport into town? **Airport bus 1195 plus metro Line 2** is currently the cheapest combination: **a 450-rouble ticket + a metro fare of about 79–90 roubles**, and you are in the city centre in about an hour.

## The whole route

### 1. Clear immigration, collect your bags, find the right exit

Once you are through customs with your luggage, leave through the exit **next to the YooMoney counter**. This exit is a bit out of the way, so take care not to head off towards another terminal.

![After customs, take the exit beside the YooMoney counter](./images/1.webp)

### 2. Turn right outside and you are at the stop

Turn **right** as you come out and there is the airport bus stop, stand number **C1**, with "от Мс Ховрино" on the sign — meaning "from Khovrino metro station".

![Turn right outside and you are at stop C1](./images/2.webp)

![The stand layout in the arrivals area (Прилёт)](./images/3.webp)

### 3. Buy your ticket from the conductor

A uniformed **conductor** on the platform will come over and sell you a ticket: **450 roubles per person**, and you can **pay by card or in cash**.

> **Large notes are not accepted** — conductors often cannot change 1,000 and 5,000 rouble notes, so get some small change beforehand.

![The conductor: card or cash, no large notes](./images/4.webp)

### 4. Ride bus 1195 all the way to the terminus

The **red bus 1195** runs straight to the end of the line, and **the terminus is the Line 2 metro station**, so there is no need to worry about which stop to get off at.

![The 1195 terminus is the Line 2 metro station](./images/5.webp)

![The airport bus stands at night](./images/6.webp)

### 5. Into the metro: all luggage goes through the scanner

At the terminus, follow the crowd into the metro station. **Every piece of luggage goes through the security scanner**, and big suitcases have to be lifted onto the belt.

![The luggage scanner at the metro entrance](./images/7.webp)

### 6. Tap in at the gates — one card covers two people

The metro gates accept cards (a Troika card or a single ticket). **One card can be tapped twice in a row**: let the first person through, then tap again for your companion.

![Tapping through the gates: one card can be used for two people](./images/8.webp)

![The Line 2 concourse](./images/9.webp)

## Costs

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Airport bus 1195 | 450 roubles per person |
| Metro, one way | about 79 roubles (the author is not certain; a cash single is usually 90 roubles — check the ticket machine) |
| **Total** | **about 530–540 roubles per person** |

## Practical tips

- **Navigation goes haywire on this stretch**: while the bus is moving, your phone's position jumps around constantly. Don't panic — **just get off with the majority**, because the terminus is the metro station.
- This bus is actually **not especially cheap** (450 roubles is close to airport express coach pricing), but it is far cheaper than a taxi and **barely affected by traffic**.
- With a lot of luggage, keep in mind the lifting at the bus luggage hold and at the metro scanner.
- Buy your metro ticket or top up your Troika card **once you reach the metro station** — there is no need to queue at the airport.
