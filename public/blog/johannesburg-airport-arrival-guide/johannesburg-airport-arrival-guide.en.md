On a first trip to South Africa, the thing you dread most is arriving with your documents and your process not sorted out. This guide sets out four things clearly: **preparation before you fly, the immigration process, getting from the airport into the city, and the traps to avoid**.

## Before you fly: nothing here can be skipped

1. **Sort out your visa/ETA in advance**: Chinese passport holders need a **visa arranged beforehand** to enter South Africa. Apply online through the official channel, one to two weeks before departure, so nothing derails the trip — **never trust an agent who offers "no visa needed, just pay"**.
2. **Have your supporting documents ready**: hotel booking, travel itinerary and return ticket — **print every one of them on paper**; do not keep them only on your phone. Handing them over at a spot check is far less hassle.
3. **Back up your passport properly**: your passport must be valid for **more than 6 months** and undamaged; photocopy and photograph the photo page and the visa page, and **keep the original and the copies in separate places**, so that a loss can be fixed quickly.

## The immigration process, in order

Once you land at O. R. Tambo International Airport (JNB):

1. **Fill in the arrival card on the plane**: write **the hotel address and telephone number** clearly, in English, in legible handwriting.
2. **Passport control**: **phones are strictly forbidden**. The officer will ask the purpose of your visit and how long you are staying — answer honestly and hand over your documents when asked, and keep an eye on your hand luggage.
3. **Customs allowances**: cigarettes ≤ 200; wine ≤ 2 litres, other alcohol ≤ 1 litre; anything above that must be declared. After you clear, **check the entry stamp and the permitted length of stay**, and sort out any problem on the spot.
4. **Yellow fever vaccination**: not required on a direct flight from China; only if you **transit an infected area** do you need the yellow card showing vaccination 10 days beforehand.

## Airport to the city: four ways

| Option | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Gautrain (first choice)** | Safe and quick, about 15 minutes to Sandton, fare around 158 rand, running roughly 05:30–20:30 |
| **Ride-hailing apps** | Order Uber or Bolt in the app and go to the airport's **designated pickup point**; refuse the touts |
| **Airport pickup / official shuttle** | Book a hotel pickup in advance, or take an official shuttle such as EZ Shuttle — trouble-free and safe |
| **Self-drive (choose with care)** | Needs a certified translation of your licence plus dual authentication; South Africa drives on the left with right-hand drive, so not recommended for novices |

> Gautrain frequencies and the last train do change, so check the official website's timetable for the day before you travel — **a late flight will very likely miss it**, and calling a car is the steadier choice then.

## Practical local tips

- **Money and tipping**: the currency is the South African rand (ZAR). **The airport rate is poor, so change only a small amount of cash**; restaurants generally expect a **10%–15%** tip, and porters **5–10 rand per bag**.
- **Safety first**: do not go out alone at night and do not show your valuables; when driving, keep the windows up and the doors locked, and avoid isolated areas.
- **Phones**: buy a Vodacom or MTN SIM card at the airport — better value than international roaming, and **your passport is all you need**.
- **Emergency numbers**: police **10111**; ambulance **999** or **082911**; if you run into trouble you can also contact the Chinese embassy or consulates in South Africa.

## Six traps you must watch out for

1. **Never get into an unlicensed taxi at the airport** — use official transport only.
2. **Carry little cash**, and lock valuables in the hotel safe.
3. Do not engage with strangers who approach you, and stay away from crowded, confusing areas.
4. **Fruit, meat and seeds may not be brought in** — customs checks are strict.
5. Stay in **safe areas such as Sandton**; do not chase a cheap price into a remote neighbourhood.
6. Fill in your ETA/visa information **truthfully** — false details mean refused entry.
