Once you land at Cape Town Airport, two things matter most: **getting cash** and **finding the Uber pickup**. Neither is in a spot you would notice at a glance, so this guide lays out the routes.

## Money exchange: start with Standard Bank

**Standard Bank offers a good rate and essentially no commission**, which makes it one of the better-value options in the airport.

![Standard Bank counter](./images/1.webp)

### The ATM is not at the international arrivals exit

This is the easiest trap to fall into: **there is no ATM near the international arrivals exit**.

Here is the correct route:

1. Coming out of international arrivals, **walk straight ahead towards the outside** until you reach **P2**, where you will see signs for **Domestic Arrivals**;
2. **The ATM is on the left-hand side at P3 Domestic Arrivals.**

![Following the signs to Domestic Arrivals](./images/2.webp)

![ATM and exchange counter next to P3 Domestic Arrivals](./images/3.webp)

### A real UnionPay withdrawal

The author withdrew cash from the airport ATM with a China Merchants Bank UnionPay debit card: **5,000 ZAR withdrawn, 2,073.52 CNY debited**, an effective UnionPay rate of about **1 ZAR = 0.4147 CNY** (roughly 1 CNY ≈ 2.41 ZAR).

![UnionPay withdrawal details: 5,000 ZAR = 2,073.52 CNY](./images/4.webp)

That is a normal rate, and **withdrawing directly with a UnionPay card saves you the hassle of carrying a large amount of foreign cash**. Before you withdraw, check that your card is enabled for overseas cash withdrawals and whether a fee applies.

![Rate conversion reference: 5,000 ZAR is about 2,058 CNY](./images/5.webp)

## Ride-hailing: the Uber pickup is at P6-7

**The Uber pickup point is at the P6-7 exit.** Just follow the **Parkade 1** signs to get there, and **set the same spot in the app** — otherwise it is easy to be taken to a different parking garage.

![Direction of the airport exit](./images/6.webp)

![Following the Parkade 1 signs](./images/7.webp)

## Connectivity: an eSIM is less hassle

The author bought an eSIM on an e-commerce platform: **30 days, calls included, around 180 CNY**. Activate it on landing and you are set — no queue for a physical SIM.

## Summary

| What to do | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Exchange money at Standard Bank | Inside the airport, good rate, no commission |
| Withdraw cash from an ATM | Straight out to P2, then **left-hand side at P3 Domestic Arrivals** |
| Catch an Uber | **P6-7 exit**, following the Parkade 1 signs |
| Phone card | Buy an eSIM in advance, or get a physical SIM at the airport counter |

> Plenty of places in South Africa still take cash (tips, street stalls, some car parks), so keep a little small change on you; pay by card for larger purchases.
