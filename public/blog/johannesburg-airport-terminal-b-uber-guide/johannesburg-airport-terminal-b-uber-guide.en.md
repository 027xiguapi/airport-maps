Most guides to catching a ride at Johannesburg airport that you find online are written about **Terminal A**, but domestic flights arrive at **Terminal B**. This article fills in the full route for domestic arrivals at Terminal B.

## Step one: pick the right terminal when you book

After you enter your destination in Uber, **the pickup point must be set to "Terminal B - Domestic Arrivals"**.

The app defaults to pinning your location at **Terminal A**. The two are not far apart, but dragging a suitcase the long way round is no fun at all. Check it before you request the ride.

## The walk in six steps

Uber's in-app walking directions are very detailed; here they are, the six steps in order.

### 1. From baggage claim up to level 1 (L1)

Walking out of baggage claim, **turn right** to the escalators and ride up to level 1.

![Turn right out of baggage claim and take the escalator to level 1](./images/1.webp)

### 2. On level 1, turn right towards the restaurants

At the top of the escalator keep going straight, then **turn right** and head towards the restaurants.

![On level 1, turn right and head towards the restaurants](./images/2.webp)

### 3. Take the skywalk to Parkade 2

Cross the skywalk beside the SAPS desk (on your right) to the **Parkade 2** car park.

![Cross the skywalk beside the SAPS desk](./images/3.webp)

### 4. Enter Parkade 2, then turn right

Follow the pedestrian crossing and turn right into Parkade 2.

![Inside Parkade 2, follow the crossing and turn right](./images/4.webp)

### 5. Straight through to pillar 2E

Go straight across the pedestrian crossing to **pillar 2E**.

![Cross the pedestrian crossing to pillar 2E](./images/5.webp)

### 6. Meet your driver at pillar 2E

Meet your driver at the designated pickup point in front of pillar 2E.

![Meet your driver in front of pillar 2E](./images/6.webp)

## What the ride costs

From Terminal B to **Sandton (near Sandton City) it came to about 350 rand** — not cheap.

The author arrived at midday and left at midday the next day, so only 24 hours on the ground. If your trip keeps the same short-stop rhythm, **renting a car for a day is often less hassle** — collect it near the airport and drop it off on your way out.

## A few things to know

- The pickup point is **inside the car park**, not at the terminal door; just follow Uber's walking directions.
- Uber pickup points in South Africa are usually marked with pillar numbers, and **noting the pillar number** (2E here) is more accurate than describing where you are.
- If you land at night, request the ride in the app and walk to the designated pickup point rather than accepting offers from touts outside the arrivals hall.
