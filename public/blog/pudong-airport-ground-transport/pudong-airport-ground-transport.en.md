The National Day holiday is nearly here, so anyone planning a trip to Shanghai, pay attention.

Arriving at Pudong International Airport, how do you get quickly from Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 onto the metro, into a taxi or onto a bus into the city? Here is the transport guide — the **Pudong International Airport edition**. Take a look ↓

## Pudong International Airport — Terminal 2

### Where things are

The arrivals level of Terminal 2 has **three exits**: **on the second floor**, the international / Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan arrivals exit and the domestic arrivals exit; **on the first floor**, the domestic arrivals exit.

Diagram ↓

![Terminal 2 arrivals layout](./images/3.webp)

The international / Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan arrivals exit on the second floor ↓

![International, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan arrivals exit, floor 2](./images/4.webp)

The domestic arrivals exit on the second floor ↓

![Domestic arrivals exit, floor 2](./images/5.webp)

The domestic arrivals exit on the first floor ↓

![Domestic arrivals exit, floor 1](./images/6.webp)

### Metro and rail

**Leaving from the second-floor international / Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan exit or the domestic exit**

Follow the signs and walk straight on towards Wuzhou Middle Road ↓

![Following the signs towards Wuzhou Middle Road](./images/8.webp)

![Following the signs towards Wuzhou Middle Road](./images/9.webp)

Follow the signs into the transfer hall: on one side is the **Metro Line 2** entrance, on the other the **Maglev** entrance ↓

![The transfer hall: Metro Line 2 and Maglev entrances](./images/10.webp)

![The transfer hall: Metro Line 2 and Maglev entrances](./images/11.webp)

![The transfer hall: Metro Line 2 and Maglev entrances](./images/12.webp)

If you are taking the **Airport Link** city rail line, follow the signs straight ahead and take the lift up to the transfer hall ↓

![Signs to the Airport Link](./images/13.webp)

![Signs to the Airport Link](./images/14.webp)

![Signs to the Airport Link](./images/15.webp)

![Signs to the Airport Link](./images/16.webp)

**Leaving from the first-floor domestic arrivals exit**

**Take the lift up to the second floor first**, then follow the same route to Metro Line 2, the Maglev or the Airport Link ↓

![Take the lift to floor 2 for the metro](./images/17.webp)

### Taxi

Terminal 2's taxi pick-up point is **outside the terminal on the first floor**, with 26 loading bays.

**Leaving from the second-floor international / Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan exit or the domestic exit**

Follow the signs and take the lift down to the first floor ↓

![Take the lift down to floor 1](./images/19.webp)

![Take the lift down to floor 1](./images/20.webp)

Go through **Gate 26** to reach the taxi pick-up point ↓

![Through Gate 26 to the taxi rank](./images/21.webp)

![Through Gate 26 to the taxi rank](./images/22.webp)

![Through Gate 26 to the taxi rank](./images/23.webp)

**Leaving from the first-floor domestic arrivals exit**

Go through **Gate 26** to reach the taxi pick-up point ↓

![Through Gate 26 to the taxi rank](./images/24.webp)

### Buses

**Leaving from the second-floor international / Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan exit or the domestic exit**

Follow the signs and walk straight on towards Wuzhou Middle Road ↓

![Following the signs towards Wuzhou Middle Road](./images/26.webp)

![Following the signs towards Wuzhou Middle Road](./images/27.webp)

Follow the signs and take the lift to the bus waiting area ↓

![Take the lift to the bus waiting area](./images/28.webp)

![Take the lift to the bus waiting area](./images/29.webp)

![Take the lift to the bus waiting area](./images/30.webp)

Terminal 2's bus waiting area serves Airport Line 4, Airport Line 7, Airport Line 8, Airport Ring Line 1 and the Shouhang overnight service (Terminal 2 to Hongqiao Hub East Transport Centre, first and last departures 23:03 to 05:33 the next morning).

![Buses from Terminal 2](./images/31.webp)

![Buses from Terminal 2](./images/32.webp)

**Leaving from the first-floor domestic arrivals exit**

**Take the lift up to the second floor first**, then follow the route above to the bus waiting area ↓

![Take the lift to floor 2 for the buses](./images/33.webp)

## Pudong International Airport — Terminal 1

### Where things are

The arrivals level of Terminal 1 has **two exits**, both **on the first floor**: the international / Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan arrivals exit and the domestic arrivals exit.

Diagram ↓

![Terminal 1 arrivals layout](./images/36.webp)

The international / Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan arrivals exit on the first floor ↓

![International, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan arrivals exit, floor 1](./images/37.webp)

The domestic arrivals exit on the first floor ↓

![Domestic arrivals exit, floor 1](./images/38.webp)

### Metro and rail

If you arrive at Terminal 1 and want Metro Line 2, the Maglev or the Airport Link, take the lift **up to the second floor** ↓

![Take the lift up to floor 2](./images/40.webp)

Follow the signs and walk straight on through the door ↓

![Follow the signs through the door](./images/41.webp)

![Follow the signs through the door](./images/42.webp)

Step into the transfer hall: on one side is the **Metro Line 2** entrance, on the other the **Maglev** entrance ↓

![The transfer hall: Metro Line 2 and Maglev entrances](./images/43.webp)

![The transfer hall: Metro Line 2 and Maglev entrances](./images/44.webp)

![The transfer hall: Metro Line 2 and Maglev entrances](./images/45.webp)

If you are taking the **Airport Link** city rail line, follow the signs and take the lift to the transfer hall ↓

![Take the lift to the transfer hall for the Airport Link](./images/46.webp)

![Take the lift to the transfer hall for the Airport Link](./images/47.webp)

![Take the lift to the transfer hall for the Airport Link](./images/48.webp)

### Taxi

Terminal 1's taxi pick-up point is **outside the terminal on the first floor**, with 20 loading bays.

![Terminal 1 taxi rank](./images/50.webp)

![Terminal 1 taxi rank](./images/51.webp)

Leaving by the **domestic arrivals exit**, cross to **Gate 4** to reach the taxi pick-up point ↓

![Gate 4 to the taxi rank](./images/52.webp)

Leaving by the **international / Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan arrivals exit**, cross to **Gate 8** to reach the taxi pick-up point ↓

![Gate 8 to the taxi rank](./images/53.webp)

### Buses

Terminal 1's bus waiting area is **outside the terminal on the first floor**, serving Airport Line 4, Airport Line 7, Airport Line 8, Airport Ring Line 1 and the Shouhang overnight service (Terminal 1 to Hongqiao Hub East Transport Centre, first and last departures 23:00 to 05:30 the next morning).

![Buses from Terminal 1](./images/55.webp)

![Buses from Terminal 1](./images/56.webp)

Leaving by the **domestic arrivals exit**, cross to **Gate 4** to reach the bus waiting area ↓

![Gate 4 to the bus waiting area](./images/57.webp)

Leaving by the **international / Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan arrivals exit**, cross to **Gate 8** to reach the bus waiting area ↓

![Gate 8 to the bus waiting area](./images/58.webp)
