Changsha Huanghua International Airport (airport code **CSX**) sits in Huanghua town, Changsha county, and is a major international gateway for central China, with two terminals in service (T1 and T2). From the city, the **Changsha maglev** connects to Changsha South high-speed railway station and **Metro line 6** runs to the airport.

So which international and regional destinations can you reach **non-stop** from Changsha? This list is compiled from the schedules the original material published for the **2025–26 winter season (February–March 2026)**: **15 routes** covering South-East Asia, North-East Asia, Europe and Africa. **Routes and frequencies change with each season**, so confirm the current timetable with the airline before you book — how to do that is at the end.

## 2. Route by route

### Myanmar: Yangon (RGN)

Myanmar Airways International flies Changsha–Yangon as **8M721**: leaving Changsha at 22:35 and landing at 00:45 the next day, 3 hours 40 minutes in the air, **twice a week (Tuesday and Saturday)**. It is an overnight hop, which sets you up for Shwedagon Pagoda first thing the next morning.

### Thailand: Bangkok (DMK and BKK) and Phuket (HKT)

Thailand has the densest service of any destination from Changsha, split across three airports:

- **Bangkok Don Mueang (DMK)**: Thai AirAsia and Thai Lion Air, flights **FD481 and SL935**, leaving Changsha at 12:55 and arriving 15:20, or leaving 14:35 and arriving 17:05, 3 hours 30 minutes, **twice daily**;
- **Bangkok Suvarnabhumi (BKK)**: XiamenAir, flight **MF8643**, leaving Changsha at 08:30 and arriving 11:05, 3 hours 35 minutes, **daily until 28 February 2026** — a morning departure that puts you in Bangkok at midday;
- **Phuket (HKT)**: Thai Lion Air, flight **SL969**, leaving Changsha 17:20 and arriving 20:35, 4 hours 15 minutes, **three times a week until 2 March 2026**, straight to the islands.

### United Kingdom: London (LHR)

Hainan Airlines flies Changsha–London Heathrow as **HU7963**: leaving Changsha 13:45 and landing at Heathrow at 17:35 local time, 11 hours 50 minutes, **twice a week (Monday and Friday)**. It is Changsha's only direct route to Europe — the one to know if you are studying or working in the UK.

![Poster for the Changsha–London route: Hainan Airlines HU7963, 13:45 to 17:35 at Heathrow, twice weekly (Monday, Friday)](./images/4.webp)

### South Korea: Seoul (ICN)

China Southern, Korean Air and Asiana all fly Changsha–Seoul Incheon, flights **CZ3065 / KE146 / KE820 / OZ322**: leaving Changsha 12:55 and arriving 17:00, about 4 hours, **twice daily**. With three carriers on the route, there is room to compare fares and change dates.

![Poster for the Changsha–Seoul route: China Southern, Korean Air and Asiana, 12:55 to 17:00 at Incheon, twice daily](./images/5.webp)

### Hong Kong, China: Hong Kong (HKG)

Cathay Pacific flies Changsha–Hong Kong as **CX943 and CX927**: leaving Changsha 21:15 and arriving 22:55, 1 hour 40 minutes, **daily until 28 March 2026**. The late arrival lines up neatly with Cathay's long-haul departures the next morning.

![Poster for the Changsha–Hong Kong route: Cathay Pacific CX943 and CX927, 21:15 to 22:55, daily until 28 Mar 2026](./images/6.webp)

### Malaysia: Kuala Lumpur (KUL)

China Southern, AirAsia X, XiamenAir and Batik Air fly Changsha–Kuala Lumpur, flights **CZ5049, D7353, MF8015 and OD643**: leaving Changsha 11:50 and arriving 16:20, 4 hours 30 minutes, **2–4 times daily**. With that much service, changing dates at short notice is rarely a problem.

![Poster for the Changsha–Kuala Lumpur route: China Southern, AirAsia X, XiamenAir and Batik Air, 11:50 to 16:20, 2–4 daily](./images/7.webp)

### Singapore: Singapore (SIN)

China Southern, China Eastern and Scoot fly Changsha–Singapore, flights **CZ5021, MU2045 and TR125**: leaving Changsha 00:05 and arriving 04:40, 4 hours 35 minutes, **twice daily**. These are red-eye departures, which land you in time for a full first day.

![Poster for the Changsha–Singapore route: China Southern, China Eastern and Scoot, 00:05 to 04:40 at Changi, twice daily](./images/8.webp)

### Cambodia: Phnom Penh (KTI)

Cambodia Airways and Sky Angkor Airways fly Changsha–Phnom Penh, flights **KR942 and ZA638**: leaving Changsha 12:15 and arriving 14:30, 3 hours 15 minutes, **five times a week**. An afternoon departure with an afternoon arrival — the most civilised timing in this list.

![Poster for the Changsha–Phnom Penh route: Cambodia Airways KR942 and Sky Angkor Airways ZA638, 12:15 to 14:30, five weekly](./images/9.webp)

### Indonesia: Jakarta (CGK)

Citilink flies Changsha–Jakarta as **QG8837**: leaving Changsha 01:30 and arriving 06:20, 5 hours 50 minutes, **weekly on Wednesdays between 11 February and 4 March 2026**.

- **Ho Chi Minh City**: China Southern, flight **CZ6061**, leaving Changsha 23:20 and arriving 01:55 the next day, 3 hours 35 minutes, **three times a week until 28 February 2026 (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday)**;
- **Hanoi**: China Southern, flight **CZ8359**, leaving Changsha 18:00 and arriving 19:20, 2 hours 20 minutes, **five times a week** — the shortest flight in this list, out before dinner and in after it.

## 3. Schedules change: how to check before you fly

The frequencies and times above come from the original material's **2025–26 winter season (February–March 2026)** schedule, and several of them carry an explicit "until" date. **Airlines revise routes and timings every season (at the end of March and the end of October)**, and a route can change airline or airport — note that Bangkok has two airports, **BKK (Suvarnabhumi)** and **DMK (Don Mueang)**, about 30 km apart.

Before you travel:

1. Search the **airline's own website or app** for your exact date rather than relying on a "so many flights a week" summary;
2. Cross-check the flight number, terminal and times on a **booking site**;
3. Re-check the **flight status** the day before — delays, terminal changes and equipment swaps all affect where you check in;
4. Read the airport code carefully: BKK and DMK are not interchangeable for connections or pick-ups.
