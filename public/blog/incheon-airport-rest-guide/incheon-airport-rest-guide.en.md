**Free 24-hour rest guide**

_REST & RELAX_

On a layover at Incheon you do not have to spend anything to find a comfortable corner and lie down. Whether you are connecting for a few hours or staying overnight, both T1 and T2 keep free rest areas open around the clock — and if you want a shower before the next flight, the airport runs paid Shower Rooms as well.

This guide covers the three free areas (Nap, Cozy and Relax Zones) and the paid Shower Room: where each one is, what it offers and when it is open. Worth saving before you fly.

## At a glance: which of the three free zones to pick

All of them sit on the fourth floor (4F), a few minutes' walk apart. The only question is what you want to do:

| Zone | Where | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nap Zone | T1, 4F east side, near gate 25; T2, 4F east side, near gate 268 and 4F west side, near gate 231 | Lying down to sleep |
| Cozy Zone | T1, 4F east side, transit rest area | Working or studying |
| Relax Zone | T1, 4F west side, near gate 43 and 4F east side, near gate 11 | Fresh air and a drink |

## 01 Nap Zone: the free area built for sleep

A very long layover and you want to lie flat? The airport's free Nap Zones are open to you, with reclining benches and partitions between the bays for far more privacy.

![Nap Zone at Incheon Airport](./images/3.webp)

📍 T1, 4F east side, near gate 25

📍 T2, 4F east side, near gate 268

📍 T2, 4F west side, near gate 231

👉 **The experience**: reclining benches, with partitions between the bays for far more privacy.

💡 **Facilities**: every zone has power outlets — Korean round-pin sockets, so bring an adapter.

💡 **Stay warm**: cover yourself with a thick coat or a blanket before you fall asleep.

## 02 Cozy Zone: the work-and-study area

For transit passengers who need to work or study, this is the one to pick. The Cozy Zone is open-plan, with sofa chairs and round tables as well as long desks with power sockets, and seats are usually available. Open free 24 hours.

![Cozy Zone at Incheon Airport](./images/4.webp)

📍 **Where**: T1, 4F east side, transit rest area.

✨ Open-plan, with sofa chairs and round tables, plus long desks with power sockets.

✨ Seats are usually available; open free 24 hours.

## 03 Relax Zone: sofas and room to breathe

Also a free 24-hour open area, with sofa chairs and power outlets and excellent air circulation; the bar next door sells drinks. If you want to stand up and move around a little, it beats the gate seating.

![Relax Zone at Incheon Airport](./images/5.webp)

📍 T1, 4F west side, near gate 43

📍 T1, 4F east side, near gate 11

## 04 Shower Room: paid, and not open around the clock

Long layover and you would like to freshen up? Both terminals have **paid Shower Rooms** next to the Nap Zones, sold in 30-minute sessions. Note that they are not open 24 hours — last entry is 22:30.

![Shower Room at Incheon Airport](./images/6.webp)

⭐ **Where**:

📍 T1, 4F east side, near gate 25

📍 T1, 4F west side, near gate 29

📍 T2, 4F east side, near gate 268

📍 T2, 4F west side, near gate 231

💰 **Price**: USD 6 per transit passenger (card or cash), for 30 minutes — overtime is charged extra.

⏰ **Opening hours**: 05:00–23:00. The Shower Rooms are _not_ open around the clock; last entry is 22:30.

🧴 **Provided**: body wash, shampoo and conditioner, toothbrush and toothpaste, hairdryer.

## Layover tips

- Check which terminal you are in first: all three free zones and the shower rooms exist in both T1 and T2, but at different gates.
- Bring a plug adapter — most sockets are Korean round-pin, and you will want it for charging and for the hairdryer.
- If you plan to sleep, keep your thick coat or blanket within reach so you do not get cold overnight.
- Plan the shower in advance: last entry is 22:30, so on a tight connection it is safer to shower first and rest afterwards.

_💡 A tip: if your layover is long, plan your rest and shower times in advance and bring a plug adapter. Enjoy your connection!_
