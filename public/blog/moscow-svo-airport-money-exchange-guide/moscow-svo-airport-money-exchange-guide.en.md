![The Сбер (Sberbank) branch at Terminal B of Sheremetyevo Airport](./images/0.webp)

When you change money into roubles at SVO, two decisions — **which counter and how much** — determine whether you quietly lose tens or even a hundred-odd yuan for nothing.

## Best rate: Сбер (Sberbank)

At Terminal B, find this **Сбер (Sberbank)** branch: **its rate is the best in the airport**, around **1 CNY = 11.x roubles**.

![The Сбер branch inside the airport, its green light box hard to miss](./images/1.webp)

## Counters to avoid

The other currency exchange points in the airport are clearly worse, only about **1 CNY = 10 roubles** — more than 10% below Сбер.

![The posted rates at counters like this one are noticeably poor, so compare before you change](./images/2.webp)

> Before you change money, look at the two columns on the rate board, "buy" and "sell", and **compare only the direction you will actually use**. Do not be misled by the big advertising type.

![Ticket and exchange counters inside Terminal B](./images/3.webp)

## How much to change: enough for the fare

**Do not change too much at the airport** — **about 500 CNY** covers the transport into the city centre.

Change larger amounts once you are in the city:

- **Go to a VTB Bank branch** and change **CNY cash**; that gives the best rate.
- No CNY cash? Use a **UnionPay debit card** to withdraw at an ATM of the **Russian Agricultural Bank (Россельхозбанк)**.
- The yellow ATM with the "hammer" logo in the sixth photo (the author calls it the "hammer bank") **works too**, only the rate is a little worse.

![The arrivals hall in Terminal B](./images/4.webp)

![The yellow "hammer"-logo ATM: UnionPay withdrawals work, but the rate is slightly worse](./images/5.webp)

## Why other banks do not work

Because of sanctions, most Russian banks **cannot take UnionPay, Visa or Mastercard**. So the number of machines that will actually give you cash in Russia is smaller than you would expect — **when you find one that works, remember where it is**; do not wait until your cash runs out to start looking.

## Summary

| Place | Method | Rate / advice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SVO Airport | Сбер (Sberbank) | About 1 CNY ≈ 11.x roubles, the best in the airport |
| SVO Airport | Other exchange counters | About 1 CNY ≈ 10 roubles, **avoid** |
| City centre | VTB Bank branch (CNY cash) | Best rate |
| City centre | Russian Agricultural Bank / "hammer" ATM (UnionPay) | Fine as a backup, slightly worse rate |

**Suggested amount**: about 500 CNY equivalent at the airport, the rest once you are in the city.
