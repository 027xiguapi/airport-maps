This page explains how World Airport Maps (the "site") handles information when you visit.

## What we collect

**The site requires no account and collects no personally identifying information** such as names, email addresses or phone numbers. You can browse the entire airport directory anonymously.

We may passively collect the following **non-personal** technical information in order to tell whether the site is working:

- The page path visited, the referring page and time spent
- Browser type, operating system, interface language and screen size
- Approximate location (country / city level, inferred from IP address; the full IP address is not retained)

## Cookies and local storage

The site itself uses **essential cookies only**:

| Name | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `preferred-locale` | Remembers the interface language you chose so your next visit opens in it | 1 year |

Third-party ad services on the page may set cookies of their own (see "Third-party services" below); those are not set by the site. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. With cookies blocked your language preference will not be remembered, but **no airport content becomes unavailable** — every language version is directly addressable (`/zh/`, `/en/`). Ads still display, though they may be less relevant.

## Third-party services

- **Fonts**: web fonts are served by Google Fonts. Loading them makes a request to that service, and its privacy policy applies.
- **Advertising**: the site carries ads served by third-party ad networks (Google AdSense and Adsterra) to fund its operation. Those services may use cookies, web beacons or similar device identifiers, combined with browsing records (including a coarse location inferred from your IP address), to serve and measure ads. Their handling of ad data is governed by their own terms and privacy policies; you can manage personalised advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). Ad slots are loaded by the third party, and the site cannot read or control the specific data they collect.
- **Analytics**: where analytics are enabled, we review aggregate figures only; they are not used to identify individuals.

We do not sell, rent or trade visitor data.

## External links

Airport pages may link to airports' own sites or to third-party transport operators. Their content and privacy practices are their own responsibility and are not covered by this policy.

## Accuracy of the data

The terminal layout diagrams are generated automatically to illustrate the overall structure of an airport and how its terminals relate to one another. They are not surveyed floor plans. Terminal counts, gates, airlines and transport details are compiled from public sources and change as airports are rebuilt. **Always confirm travel arrangements against information published by the airport.**

## Children's privacy

The site is intended for general travellers, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your rights

Because the site stores no personally identifying information, there is normally no personal profile to export or delete. If you believe the site has retained personal information about you in some way, please contact us and we will investigate.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page changes with it. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, please reach us through the contact details published on this site.
