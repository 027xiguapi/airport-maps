## Disclaimer

**This site is a planning aid, not an official source of information.**

1. **The terminal diagrams are illustrative.** They are generated from terminal counts, gate counts and the ground transport node to convey the overall structure of an airport and how its terminals relate to one another. They are **not surveyed floor plans** and may differ from the real building's shape, scale and orientation.
2. **The data may be out of date.** Terminals open, close and are rebuilt, gate numbering changes, airlines move between terminals, and transport routes and operating hours change.
3. **Defer to official sources.** For gates, check-in deadlines, connection times, visa requirements and security rules, always rely on information published by the airport, your airline and the relevant authorities.
