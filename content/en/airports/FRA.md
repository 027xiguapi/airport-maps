Frankfurt Airport lies about 12 km from the city centre and handles around 61 million passengers a year, Germany's largest hub. Its structure is easy to read: **Lufthansa and Star Alliance have T1 to themselves and everyone else uses T2**, with the SkyLine people mover linking the two. More unusual still, the airport has **two railway stations** — and boarding at the wrong one is the most common mistake made here.

## The two terminals

| Code | Name | Gate areas | Main carriers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Terminal 1 | A / B / C / Z | Lufthansa and Star Alliance members |
| T2 | Terminal 2 | D / E | All other international carriers |

**How to tell where you are:** the gate letter is the concourse — A/B/C/Z are in T1, D/E in T2. Connections between Lufthansa and its partner airlines almost all stay inside T1.

## Getting between the terminals

- **SkyLine people mover** — free between T1 and T2, every few minutes.
- Within T1 the concourses are walkable from one another; Zone Z mostly handles non-Schengen flights, so watch for passport control when connecting.

## Getting into Frankfurt

- **Regional station (S-Bahn)** — underground, with commuter trains reaching the Hauptbahnhof in about 12 minutes; the first choice for the city.
- **Long-distance station (Fernbahnhof)** — an elevated station connected to T1, with direct ICE high-speed trains to Cologne, Munich, Berlin and other cities; the one to use for onward rail travel.
- **Taxi** — about 12 km, roughly 20 minutes when the traffic is clear.
- **Airport bus** — covers the city centre and the surrounding area.

## Practical tips

- The two stations are **one word apart**: for the city, take the underground regional station; for high-speed trains to other cities, the elevated long-distance one — check the station abbreviation before you head down or up.
- Lufthansa to Lufthansa inside T1 is the most relaxed; a T1 to T2 connection means adding the SkyLine wait and the walk at both ends.
- Connecting between a Schengen and a non-Schengen flight means passport control, so allow another notch of time on top.
