Dubai International Airport lies only about 15 km from the city centre and handles around 92 million passengers a year, making it the world's busiest international airport. What sets it apart is that **terminals are bound tightly to carriers**: Emirates has T3 to itself while most other international airlines use T1 — read the airline and the terminal is settled.

## The three terminals and four concourses

| Code | Name | Concourse | Main carriers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T3 | Terminal 3 | A / B / C | Emirates and some flydubai flights |
| T1 | Terminal 1 | D | All other international carriers |
| T2 | Terminal 2 | Standalone building | Mostly flydubai regional flights |

**The gate letter is the concourse, and the concourse decides whether you need the people mover:**

- **Concourse A** — T3's remote boarding concourse, reached by driverless people mover after security.
- **Concourse B** — immediately beside the T3 main building, walkable.
- **Concourse C** — the southern concourse connected to T3.
- **Concourse D** — T1's boarding concourse, reached by people mover after security in T1.

> Our diagram draws Concourses A and D as parallel bars set apart from the main buildings precisely to mark those two people-mover links.

## Transfers and connections

Dubai is the world's largest international transfer point, and Emirates connections almost all stay inside T3, where the in-terminal people mover keeps them efficient:

1. Read the gate letter to tell which concourse you are in; Concourses A and D need people-mover time built in.
2. Emirates to Emirates almost never means changing building — a connection of around 2 hours is already comfortable.
3. Connections involving T2 are the most awkward of all: T2 sits well away from T1 and T3, so confirm the transfer arrangement with your airline and allow longer.

## Getting into the city

- **Metro Red Line** — stations at both T1 and T3 and direct to the city centre, and the cheapest option; it runs until around midnight, so plan another way for a late arrival.
- **Taxi** — picks up at the terminal exits; about 20–30 minutes to the city centre.
- **Airport bus** — the lowest fare, but with limited routes and frequency; suited to travellers who are not in a hurry.

## Practical tips

- A 2–3 hour connection is the normal rhythm in Dubai, and there is no shortage of seating and food inside — with time in hand there is no need to rush.
- When your gate is in Concourse A, the people mover plus walking takes about 15 minutes, so **do not cut it fine for boarding**.
- Summer ground temperatures are extreme; around midday, prefer the Metro and indoor connections and keep open-air walking to a minimum.
