Schiphol lies about 15 km from Amsterdam's city centre and handles around 72 million passengers a year. It is one of Europe's most straightforward hubs: **nominally a single terminal**, with check-in, security and passport control all sharing the Schiphol Plaza hall and six piers (B–G) fanning out behind it. Here you will almost never end up in the wrong building — only in the wrong pier.

## One terminal, six piers

| Area | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Schiphol Plaza | The shared check-in hall; the railway and bus stations are on this level |
| B / C Piers | Mainly intra-European flights |
| D Pier | The busiest pier, with the most gates |
| E / F / G Piers | Mainly intercontinental and non-Schengen flights |

**How to work out where you are going:** the **letter of your gate is the pier**. D Pier has one exception — a set of gates on its lower level where you **board by shuttle bus**; if the screen shows a "D + number + D" style code or a gate marked bus, allow time for the shuttle.

> Our diagram draws it as a single hall with six radial piers precisely to show that a connection here never means changing buildings — only covering the distance between piers.

## Transfers and connections

- All connections are made behind the hall, without going through security or check-in again; for a connection between piers, estimate 15–25 minutes on foot.
- Flights between the Schengen and non-Schengen areas require passport control, so add that on top of your connection buffer.

## Getting into Amsterdam

- **Train** — the station sits directly beneath the terminal, about 15 minutes to Centraal with very frequent services; the model of a station built into an airport.
- **Direct intercity trains** — from the same platforms you can reach Rotterdam, The Hague, Utrecht and other cities without changing.
- **Taxi** — about 15 km, around 25 minutes outside peak traffic.
- **Hotel shuttle buses** — covering the main hotel clusters in the city.

## Practical tips

- For the city, almost always take the train: the station is directly beneath the hall, reachable by lift, and fares are far lower than a taxi.
- A very high share of the traffic here is KLM connections; a same-alliance connection usually works in 50–90 minutes, and you should allow more when changing piers.
- You can tap a contactless credit card at the ticket gates, so there is no need to queue for a ticket.
