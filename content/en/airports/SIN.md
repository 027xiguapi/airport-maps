Changi has four terminals, handles around 69 million passengers a year and sits about 20 km from the city centre. Its gates are numbered **by terminal zone**, so reading the letter prefix tells you which building you need before you set off.

## Terminals and their gate zones

| Code | Name | Gates | Gate count |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Terminal 1 | A1–A20, C1–C5 | 25 |
| T2 | Terminal 2 | E1–E25, F1–F10 | 35 |
| T3 | Terminal 3 | A1–A20, B1–B10 | 30 |
| T4 | Terminal 4 | G1–G11 | 11 |

**How the letters map to buildings:**

- **C, E, F and G** each belong to exactly one terminal (C→T1, E/F→T2, G→T4).
- **A and B appear in both T1 and T3** — the letter alone cannot tell you which terminal you need. Go by the terminal number printed on your boarding pass.

> That is exactly why it is worth writing down: at Changi, **read the terminal number first and the gate letter second**. Doing it the other way round is how people end up at the wrong building.

## Moving between terminals

- **Skytrain** — free inter-terminal shuttle, and the quickest way to change building.
- **On foot** — walkways also connect adjacent terminals and are often faster than waiting at peak times.

If your two flights are in different terminals, allow time for the change. A gate letter that jumps zones (E to G, say) means a building change plus a walk, which is the slowest kind of connection here.

## Getting into the city

- **MRT East West Line** — from Changi Airport station straight into the city, and the cheapest option.
- **Airport buses** — night routes cover the city, useful for red-eye arrivals.
- **Taxi** — separate, well-signed pickup tiers; the easiest choice late at night.
- **Skytrain** — inter-terminal only; it does not go into the city.

## Practical tips

- At only 20 km out, the MRT gets you downtown in around half an hour — you do not need to over-buffer the ground transfer.
- The terminals are a destination in themselves: with a long connection you can follow the signs into another terminal and come back to your gate.
- Arriving after the MRT stops for the night, take a taxi or a night bus route rather than trying to walk out of the airport.
