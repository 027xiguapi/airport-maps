LaGuardia Airport sits alongside Queens in New York, about 13 km from Manhattan, and handles around 30 million passengers a year. It has just completed a transformation: **Terminal B and Terminal C have been rebuilt and reopened**, with a look completely unlike a few years ago. But it is also **the only one of New York's three main airports without a direct rail link** — the city is reached by road, and that is its greatest shortcoming.

## The four terminals

| Code | Name | Main carriers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Terminal A | Small airlines and general aviation |
| B | Terminal B | Delta, Southwest, American and others (new build, opening in stages from 2022) |
| C | Terminal C | Delta's principal base (new build) |
| D | Terminal D | United, Air Canada and others (being refurbished) |

**How to work out where you are:**

- Check the operating airline: **Delta at C (some at B), Southwest and American mostly at B, United at D**.
- Free shuttle buses run between the terminals, but **some can also be reached on foot**; follow the signs on the ground.

## Transfers and connections

1. LaGuardia is dominated by origin-and-destination traffic, and **its connecting facilities fall short of JFK's**, so a transfer between separately booked tickets needs generous time.
2. An inter-terminal transfer relies on the shuttle bus; allow more than 2 hours.
3. If your itinerary needs a long-haul connection, book through JFK rather than LaGuardia.

## Getting into New York

- **Bus plus subway** — the Q70 and other bus routes connect to the subway and commuter rail; the lowest fare, and the most common substitute for rail.
- **Taxi / rideshare** — about 13 km, but congestion on the East River bridges and tunnels is severe, and **the journey can take far longer than the distance suggests**.
- **Airport buses** — link the main Manhattan hubs and hotel districts.
- **Driving** — via the Grand Central Parkway and the river crossings; not advisable at peak times.

## Practical tips

- The division of labour among New York's three airports: **JFK for long-haul international, Newark (EWR) for international and as United's hub, LaGuardia (LGA) for short-haul domestic** — when a transfer involves two of them, set aside half a day on its own.
- From LaGuardia, **always estimate by road time**, counting the queues for the bridges and tunnels, rather than pricing the trip on 13 km of distance.
- The new terminals' shops and rest areas are a great deal better, and worth leaving time to browse when you have a wait.
