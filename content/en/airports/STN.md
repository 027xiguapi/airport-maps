Stansted lies about 55 km from central London and handles around 29 million passengers a year. It is the most single-minded of London's three main airports: **it has just one terminal, and low-cost carriers dominate** — Ryanair, easyJet, Jet2 and the like make up the great majority of flights. Structurally there is no terminal-change problem to solve; the only thing to work out is the distance into town.

## A single terminal

| Area | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Main building | Check-in, security, immigration, all gates |

**Working out where you are going:** every flight is in the same building, so you simply head for your gate number. Low-cost flights often park on remote stands, so **factor the walking and the apron bus into your buffer** (on some flights you board a bus at the gate).

> Our diagram shows a single main building precisely to make the point that at Stansted there is no inter-terminal connection to worry about; the time cost lies in the direction of the city.

## Transfers and connections

1. Every connection happens inside the one building, but **Stansted is overwhelmingly a point-to-point airport** and through-ticketed connections are limited.
2. If you are connecting here on two separate tickets, allow more than 3 hours for immigration, baggage reclaim and checking in again.
3. Low-cost baggage rules and check-in deadlines are strict; do not estimate a connection here from full-service airline habits.

## Getting into London

- **Stansted Express** — about 47 minutes direct to London Liverpool Street, with frequent departures, and the main way into town.
- **Coach** — direct to Victoria, Liverpool Street and other stops; cheaper than the Express but exposed to traffic.
- **Taxi / ride-hailing** — about 55 km; expensive and with unpredictable journey times, so generally not recommended.
- **Car hire** — suited to destinations to the east, such as Cambridge.

## Practical tips

- London's three main airports divide the work clearly: **Heathrow (LHR) for full-service and long-haul, Gatwick (LGW) for leisure and long-haul, Stansted (STN) for low-cost** — always check the three-letter code when booking; they are a long way apart.
- The Express stops running relatively early, so for a late-night arrival take an airport coach or book a transfer.
- Low-cost gates can be a long walk away, so once you are through security head straight for your gate rather than lingering in the shops.
