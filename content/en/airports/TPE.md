Taoyuan is about 30 km from central Taipei and handles **around 48 million passengers a year**. It has only two terminals, and the division is easy to follow: **China Airlines and the SkyTeam group are mostly in T1, EVA Air and the Star Alliance group mostly in T2** — check the carrier and you can largely settle which building you need.

## The two terminals

| Code | Name | Main users |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Terminal 1 | China Airlines, SkyTeam members and others |
| T2 | Terminal 2 | EVA Air, Star Alliance members and others |

**Working out where you are going:** go by the terminal printed on your boarding pass; China Airlines in T1 and EVA Air in T2 is the quickest rule of thumb, and other foreign carriers are spread across both buildings.

## Transfers and connections

- **Free buggies and shuttle buses** run between T1 and T2, and the walkways connect them too; the distance is not great.
- China Airlines to China Airlines, or EVA Air to EVA Air, stays within one building and is the quickest.
- For a connection between buildings, add the shuttle and the walk to your buffer; for international-to-international connections, allow 2 hours.

## Getting to Taipei

- **Airport MRT express** — about 37 minutes direct to Taipei Main Station with no intermediate stops, and luggage racks in the cars.
- **Airport MRT all-stations service** — stops at every station along the line, about 50 minutes to Taipei Main Station, and cheaper.
- **Airport buses** — routes cover the main districts of Taipei and New Taipei, with services still running late at night.
- **Taxi** — about 40 minutes, noticeably congested at peak times, and considerably more expensive than the MRT.

## Practical tips

- The Airport MRT calls at **HSR Taoyuan station**, so taking the MRT and then switching to the high-speed rail south is the usual way to reach Taichung or Kaohsiung.
- The express and all-stations services share the Taipei Main Station terminus but stop differently, so check the front of the train and the platform screens while you wait.
- If you are connecting, confirm the terminal for both flights first — the China Airlines group and the EVA Air group are in different buildings, and this one check avoids most transfer confusion.
