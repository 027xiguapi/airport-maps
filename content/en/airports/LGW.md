Gatwick Airport lies about 45 km from central London and handles around 43 million passengers a year, making it London's second airport. It has only two terminals: **the North Terminal and the South Terminal**, joined by a **free automatic people-mover** (about 2 minutes) — the only "change of building" in the whole airport.

## The two terminals

| Code | Name | Main carriers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N | North Terminal | easyJet, TUI and other holiday and low-cost airlines |
| S | South Terminal | British Airways, Virgin and other long-haul airlines mainly |

**How to work out where you are:**

- Long-haul and full-service airlines are mostly in the **South Terminal**; charter and holiday routes are mostly in the **North Terminal**.
- The free shuttle (**Inter-terminal Transit**) between the two takes about 2 minutes and runs frequently; you can also walk, but it is not a short distance.
- The railway station is on the **South Terminal** side — passengers starting from the North Terminal must take the shuttle to the South before entering the station.

## Transfers and connections

1. First check whether both flights use the same terminal; a same-terminal connection saves the most time.
2. **An inter-terminal transfer goes landside and through security again**, and with the shuttle time on top you should allow more than 2.5 hours.
3. Gatwick is dominated by leisure and holiday traffic and has a lower share of connecting passengers than Heathrow, so when tickets are booked separately leave even more margin.

## Getting into London

- **Gatwick Express** — about 30 minutes direct to Victoria station, running frequently, and the fastest way into the city.
- **Ordinary commuter trains and Thameslink** — cover London Bridge, St Pancras and other stations at a lower fare.
- **Airport buses** — direct to Victoria and towards Heathrow, so they can bridge a transfer between the two airports.
- **Taxi / rideshare** — about 45 km; expensive and much affected by traffic.

## Practical tips

- London's three airports are far apart: **if you are transferring to Heathrow, the drive between the two can take more than 1.5 hours**, so do not treat the two as a single itinerary.
- The railway station is concentrated at the South Terminal, so from the North Terminal allow for the shuttle and the walk.
- At holiday peaks (summer and Christmas) the North Terminal is extremely busy; low-cost and charter passengers should arrive earlier still.
