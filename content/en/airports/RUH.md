King Khalid International Airport sits in Riyadh's northern outskirts, about 35 km from the city centre, and handles around 35 million passengers a year as Saudi Arabia's main gateway. **In February 2026 the airport completed the first full terminal reallocation in the forty years since it opened** — the rules for which buildings national and foreign carriers use are now completely different from older guides, so check everything against the new ones.

## The five terminals (new allocation from February 2026)

| Code | Name | New role (from February 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 / T2 | Terminals 1 and 2 | **National carriers' international flights** (Saudia, Riyadh Air and others) |
| T3 / T4 | Terminals 3 and 4 | **National carriers' domestic flights** |
| T5 | Terminal 5 | **Foreign carriers' international flights** |

**How to tell where you are going:**

- Start with the airline's nationality: **national carriers (the Saudia group) use T1/T2 for international flights and T3/T4 for domestic**; **foreign carriers' international flights are in T5**.
- The change re-splits international and domestic traffic by national versus foreign carrier, and capacity has risen accordingly (annual capacity is moving from about 42 million towards 56 million).

> Our diagram labels the five buildings by their new roles precisely to reflect that reallocation: the terminal assignments on older maps no longer apply.

## Transfers and connections

1. A Saudia-group connection is completed within the national-carrier buildings and is relatively smooth.
2. **Foreign carriers are concentrated in T5** — if one of your two flights is in T5 and the other in T1/T2, allow more than 2 hours.
3. Domestic flights are concentrated in T3/T4, so for an international-to-domestic connection, recalculate the time from the distance between the buildings.

## Getting into Riyadh

- **Taxi / ride-hailing** — about 35 km and roughly 35 minutes without traffic; the most common way in.
- **Airport buses** — cover the main districts of the city, with limited frequencies.
- **Metro** — Riyadh's metro network is open and links to areas around the airport, but a change requires a road transfer.
- **Car hire** — useful for trips to nearby cities and desert sights.

## Practical tips

- **Always check the terminal number on your boarding pass** — the February 2026 reallocation invalidated a great deal of older information; judge by the airline's nationality first, then go by the boarding pass.
- Saudi Arabia is building **King Salman International Airport** (six runways planned, with long-term capacity above 100 million passengers a year), expected to open around 2029, so the airport picture will keep changing over the coming years.
- Saudi Arabia controls incoming goods and some medicines strictly; check the customs rules in advance.
