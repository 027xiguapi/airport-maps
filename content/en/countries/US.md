The United States is the country with the most airports in this directory: 12 in total, holding 46 terminals and 1,062 gates and handling about 590 million passengers a year. They range from the world's busiest hubs to smaller regional airports.

## Atlanta

Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International (ATL) lies about 14 km from the city centre. With 7 terminals, 176 gates and about 108 million passengers a year, it is the largest airport in this directory.

## The New York area

Three major airports serve the New York area: John F. Kennedy International (JFK, 5 terminals, 120 gates, about 63 million passengers), Newark Liberty International (EWR, 3 terminals, 92 gates, about 49 million) and LaGuardia (LGA, 4 terminals, 58 gates, about 32 million), each roughly 13–24 km from the city centre. Nearby Westchester County Airport (HPN) is also covered as a smaller airport.

## East Coast and the South

Washington Dulles International (IAD) serves Washington with 5 terminals and 48 gates, about 28 million passengers a year. Miami International (MIA) serves Miami with 3 terminals and 120 gates, about 50 million passengers a year.

## The heartland

O'Hare International (ORD) serves Chicago with 4 terminals and 143 gates, about 73 million passengers a year. Will Rogers World Airport (OKC) serves Oklahoma City with a single terminal of 17 gates, about 4.5 million passengers a year.

## The West Coast

Los Angeles International (LAX) holds 6 terminals and 131 gates, about 76 million passengers a year; San Francisco International (SFO) holds 4 terminals and 83 gates, about 54 million; Seattle–Tacoma International (SEA) holds 3 terminals and 68 gates, about 52 million.
