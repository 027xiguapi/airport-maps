Sweden is covered here by a single airport: Stockholm Arlanda (ARN). It holds 4 terminals and 90 gates and handles about 25 million passengers a year — the largest Swedish airport in this directory.

## Stockholm Arlanda

Stockholm Arlanda (ARN) serves Stockholm and lies about 42 km from the city centre. Its 90 gates are spread across 4 terminals, carrying international traffic for Sweden and the wider Nordic region.
