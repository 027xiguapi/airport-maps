Saudi Arabia is covered here by a single airport: King Khalid International (RUH). It holds 5 terminals and 72 gates and handles about 40 million passengers a year — the largest Saudi airport in this directory.

## King Khalid International

King Khalid International (RUH) serves Riyadh and lies about 35 km from the city centre. Its 72 gates are spread across 5 terminals, so confirming the terminal before connecting matters especially.

The airport page carries a terminal layout diagram, gate ranges, main airlines and ground transport options; search the directory by the IATA code RUH to jump straight to it.
