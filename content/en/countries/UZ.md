Uzbekistan is covered here by a single airport: Islam Karimov Tashkent International (TAS). It holds 2 terminals and 20 gates and handles about 5 million passengers a year — the Central Asian gateway in this directory.

## Tashkent International

Tashkent International (TAS) serves Tashkent and lies about 12 km from the city centre. Its 20 gates are spread across 2 terminals; small in scale, but linking Central Asia with the main routes of Europe and Asia.
