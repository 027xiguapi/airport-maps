France is covered here by two airports, both serving Paris: Charles de Gaulle (CDG) and Orly (ORY). Together they hold 7 terminals and 180 gates and handle about 100 million passengers a year.

## Paris Charles de Gaulle

Charles de Gaulle (CDG) lies about 25 km from central Paris. Its 3 terminals hold 122 gates, and at about 68 million passengers a year it is the largest French airport covered here.

## Paris Orly

Orly (ORY) lies about 14 km from central Paris, closer in than Charles de Gaulle. Its 4 terminals hold 58 gates and it handles about 32 million passengers a year.
