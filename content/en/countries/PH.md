The Philippines is covered here by a single airport: Ninoy Aquino International (MNL). It holds 4 terminals and 70 gates and handles about 48 million passengers a year — the largest Philippine airport in this directory.

## Ninoy Aquino International

Ninoy Aquino International (MNL) serves Manila and lies only about 7 km from the city centre. Its 70 gates are spread across 4 terminals, which makes checking the terminal switch especially worthwhile when connecting.

The airport page carries a terminal layout diagram, gate ranges, main airlines and ground transport options; search the directory by the IATA code MNL to jump straight to it.
