New Zealand is covered here by a single airport: Auckland Airport (AKL). It holds 2 terminals and 35 gates and handles about 18 million passengers a year — the second-busiest airport covered in Oceania on this site, after Sydney.

## Auckland Airport

Auckland Airport (AKL) serves Auckland and lies about 21 km from the city centre. Its 35 gates are spread across 2 terminals, forming New Zealand's principal gateway.
