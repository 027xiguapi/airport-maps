Italy is covered here by two airports: Rome Fiumicino (FCO) and Milan Malpensa (MXP). Together they hold 5 terminals and 187 gates and handle about 79 million passengers a year.

## Rome Fiumicino

Rome Fiumicino (FCO) serves Rome and lies about 30 km from the city centre. Its 3 terminals hold 107 gates, and at about 50 million passengers a year it is the largest Italian airport covered here.

## Milan Malpensa

Milan Malpensa (MXP) serves Milan and lies about 50 km from the city centre. Its 2 terminals hold 80 gates and it handles about 29 million passengers a year.
