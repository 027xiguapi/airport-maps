Brazil is covered here by a single airport: São Paulo/Guarulhos International (GRU). It holds 3 terminals and 85 gates and handles about 44 million passengers a year — the busiest airport covered in South America on this site.

## São Paulo/Guarulhos International

São Paulo/Guarulhos International (GRU) serves São Paulo and lies about 25 km from the city centre. Its 85 gates are spread across 3 terminals, forming Brazil's main international gateway.
