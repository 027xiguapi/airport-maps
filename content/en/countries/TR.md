Türkiye is covered here by a single airport: Istanbul Airport (IST). It holds 143 gates under one terminal roof and handles about 79 million passengers a year — the largest single-terminal airport covered here and one of Europe's busiest hubs.

## Istanbul Airport

Istanbul Airport (IST) serves Istanbul and lies about 35 km from the city centre. All 143 gates sit within one giant terminal, gathering connecting traffic between Europe and Asia.
