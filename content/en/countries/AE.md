The UAE is covered here by a single airport: Dubai International (DXB). It holds 4 terminals and 137 gates and handles about 92 million passengers a year — the largest airport covered in the Middle East on this site.

## Dubai International

Dubai International (DXB) serves Dubai and sits only about 4 km from the city centre, making it one of the closest-to-town airports in this directory. Its 137 gates are spread across 4 terminals.

The airport page carries a terminal layout diagram, gate ranges, main airlines and ground transport options; search the directory by the IATA code DXB to jump straight to it.
