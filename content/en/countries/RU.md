Russia is covered here by a single airport: Sheremetyevo International (SVO). It holds 5 terminals and 70 gates and handles about 45 million passengers a year — the largest Russian airport in this directory.

## Sheremetyevo International

Sheremetyevo International (SVO) serves Moscow and lies about 30 km from the city centre. Its 70 gates are spread across 5 terminals, one of the highest terminal counts covered here, so confirming the terminal before connecting matters especially.

The airport page carries a terminal layout diagram, gate ranges, main airlines and ground transport options; search the directory by the IATA code SVO to jump straight to it.
