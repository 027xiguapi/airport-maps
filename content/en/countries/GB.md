The United Kingdom is covered here by three airports, all serving London: Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW) and London Stansted (STN). Together they hold 7 terminals and 341 gates and handle about 157 million passengers a year — the highest gate total of any European country in this directory, behind only the United States overall.

## Heathrow

Heathrow (LHR) lies about 24 km from central London. Its 4 terminals hold 95 gates, and at about 84 million passengers a year it is the largest UK airport covered here.

## Gatwick

Gatwick (LGW) lies about 46 km from central London. Its 2 terminals hold 159 gates — the most of any two-terminal airport in this directory — for about 45 million passengers a year.

## London Stansted

London Stansted (STN) lies about 48 km from central London, the farthest out of the three. A single terminal holds 87 gates and it handles about 28 million passengers a year.
