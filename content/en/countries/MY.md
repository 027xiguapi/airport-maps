Malaysia is covered here by a single airport: Kuala Lumpur International (KUL). It holds 3 terminals and 83 gates and handles about 47 million passengers a year — the largest Malaysian airport in this directory.

## Kuala Lumpur International

Kuala Lumpur International (KUL) serves Kuala Lumpur and lies about 45 km from the city centre. Its 83 gates are spread across 3 terminals, forming Malaysia's principal gateway.
