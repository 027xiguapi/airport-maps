Spain is covered here by two airports: Madrid–Barajas (MAD) and Barcelona–El Prat (BCN). Together they hold 6 terminals and 208 gates and handle about 118 million passengers a year.

## Madrid–Barajas

Madrid–Barajas (MAD) serves Madrid and lies about 13 km from the city centre. Its 4 terminals hold 118 gates, and at about 64 million passengers a year it is the largest Spanish airport covered here.

## Barcelona–El Prat

Barcelona–El Prat (BCN) serves Barcelona and also lies about 13 km from the city centre. Its 2 terminals hold 90 gates and it handles about 54 million passengers a year.
