Vietnam is covered here by a single airport: Tan Son Nhat International (SGN). It holds 2 terminals and 30 gates and handles about 40 million passengers a year — the largest Vietnamese airport in this directory.

## Tan Son Nhat International

Tan Son Nhat International (SGN) serves Ho Chi Minh City and lies only about 6 km from the city centre, one of the closest-to-town airports covered here. Its 30 gates are spread across 2 terminals, carrying a very large flow for an airport of this size.

The airport page carries a terminal layout diagram, gate ranges, main airlines and ground transport options; search the directory by the IATA code SGN to jump straight to it.
