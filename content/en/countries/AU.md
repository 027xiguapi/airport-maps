Australia is covered here by a single airport: Sydney Kingsford Smith (SYD). It holds 3 terminals and 85 gates and handles about 44 million passengers a year — the busiest airport covered in Oceania on this site.

## Sydney Kingsford Smith

Sydney Kingsford Smith (SYD) serves Sydney and lies about 8 km from the city centre. Its 85 gates are spread across 3 terminals, forming the main gateway into Australia.
