China is covered here by four airports: Guangzhou Baiyun International (CAN), Beijing Capital International (PEK), Shanghai Pudong International (PVG) and Hong Kong International (HKG). Together they hold 12 terminals and 273 gates and handle about 265 million passengers a year — every one of them a mega-hub above 50 million annual passengers.

## Guangzhou Baiyun International

Guangzhou Baiyun International (CAN) serves Guangzhou and lies about 28 km from the city centre. Its 2 terminals hold 48 gates, and at about 76 million passengers a year it is the busiest of the airports covered here.

## Beijing Capital International

Beijing Capital International (PEK) serves Beijing and lies about 25 km from the city centre. Its 3 terminals hold 52 gates and it handles about 67 million passengers a year.

## Shanghai Pudong International

Shanghai Pudong International (PVG) serves Shanghai and lies about 40 km from the city centre. Its 4 terminals hold 53 gates and it also handles about 67 million passengers a year.

## Hong Kong International

Hong Kong International (HKG) serves Hong Kong and lies about 34 km from the city centre. Its 3 terminals hold 120 gates — the most of the four airports covered here — for about 55 million passengers a year.
