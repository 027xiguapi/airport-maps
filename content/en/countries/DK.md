Denmark is covered here by a single airport: Copenhagen Airport Kastrup (CPH). It holds 3 terminals and 82 gates and handles about 29 million passengers a year — the busiest airport covered in the Nordic countries on this site.

## Copenhagen Airport Kastrup

Copenhagen Airport Kastrup (CPH) serves Copenhagen and lies only about 8 km from the city centre. Its 82 gates are spread across 3 terminals, carrying international traffic for Denmark and the wider Nordic region.
