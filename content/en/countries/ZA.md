South Africa is covered here by a single airport: Cape Town International (CPT). It holds 28 gates under one terminal roof and handles about 11 million passengers a year — the main gateway to southern Africa in this directory.

## Cape Town International

Cape Town International (CPT) serves Cape Town and lies about 20 km from the city centre. Its 28 gates sit within a single terminal, keeping the layout compact.

The airport page carries a terminal layout diagram, gate ranges, main airlines and ground transport options; search the directory by the IATA code CPT to jump straight to it.
