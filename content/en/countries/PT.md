Portugal is covered here by a single airport: Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS). It holds 2 terminals and 55 gates and handles about 33 million passengers a year — the largest Portuguese airport in this directory.

## Humberto Delgado Airport

Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS) serves Lisbon and lies only about 7 km from the city centre, one of the closest-to-town airports covered in Europe. Its 55 gates are spread across 2 terminals.
