Ireland is covered here by a single airport: Dublin Airport (DUB). It holds 2 terminals and 65 gates and handles about 33 million passengers a year — the largest Irish airport in this directory.

## Dublin Airport

Dublin Airport (DUB) serves Dublin and lies about 10 km from the city centre. Its 65 gates are spread across 2 terminals, forming Ireland's principal gateway.
