Indonesia is covered here by a single airport: Soekarno–Hatta International (CGK). It holds 3 terminals and 70 gates and handles about 60 million passengers a year, one of the major Southeast Asian hubs in this directory.

## Soekarno–Hatta International

Soekarno–Hatta International (CGK) serves Jakarta and lies about 20 km from the city centre. Its 70 gates are spread across 3 terminals, forming Indonesia's principal gateway.
