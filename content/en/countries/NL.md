The Netherlands is covered here by a single airport: Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS). It holds 110 gates under one terminal roof and handles about 67 million passengers a year — the largest Dutch airport in this directory and one of Europe's busiest hubs.

## Amsterdam Airport Schiphol

Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS) serves Amsterdam and lies about 9 km from the city centre. All of its gates sit within one large terminal, organised by lettered piers — third behind Istanbul and Bangkok among single-terminal airports covered here.
