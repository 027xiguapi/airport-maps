Greece is covered here by a single airport: Athens International (ATH). It holds 60 gates under one terminal roof and handles about 31 million passengers a year — the only Greek airport in this directory and a major hub for south-eastern Europe.

## Athens International

Athens International (ATH) serves Athens and lies about 27 km from the city centre. All 60 gates sit within a single terminal, keeping the layout compact.

The airport page carries a terminal layout diagram, gate ranges, main airlines and ground transport options; search the directory by the IATA code ATH to jump straight to it.
