Egypt is covered here by a single airport: Cairo International (CAI). It holds 3 terminals and 60 gates and handles about 27 million passengers a year — the largest airport covered in North Africa on this site.

## Cairo International

Cairo International (CAI) serves Cairo and lies about 22 km from the city centre. Its 60 gates are spread across 3 terminals, forming Egypt's principal air gateway.
