Austria is covered here by a single airport: Vienna International (VIE). It holds 3 terminals and 95 gates and handles about 31 million passengers a year, making it one of the major Central European hubs in this directory.

## Vienna International

Vienna International (VIE) serves Vienna and lies about 18 km from the city centre. Its 95 gates are spread across 3 terminals, carrying international traffic for Austria and the surrounding region.
