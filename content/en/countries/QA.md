Qatar is covered here by a single airport: Hamad International (DOH). It holds 2 terminals and 120 gates and handles about 46 million passengers a year, one of the major Middle Eastern hubs in this directory.

## Hamad International

Hamad International (DOH) serves Doha and lies about 12 km from the city centre. Its 120 gates sit within 2 terminals, among the largest two-terminal airports here alongside Hong Kong.
