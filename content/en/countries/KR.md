South Korea is covered here by a single airport: Incheon International (ICN). It holds 2 terminals and 113 gates and handles about 73 million passengers a year — the largest Korean airport in this directory and a major Northeast Asian hub.

## Incheon International

Incheon International (ICN) serves Seoul and lies about 49 km from the city centre. Its 113 gates sit within just 2 terminals, carrying a very large flow for a two-terminal airport.
