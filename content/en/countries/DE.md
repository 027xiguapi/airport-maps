Germany is covered here by two airports: Frankfurt Airport (FRA) and Munich Airport (MUC). Together they hold 5 terminals and 254 gates and handle about 96 million passengers a year — among the highest gate totals of any country in this directory.

## Frankfurt Airport

Frankfurt Airport (FRA) serves Frankfurt and lies about 12 km from the city centre. Its 3 terminals hold 144 gates, and at about 59 million passengers a year it is the largest German airport covered here, with a gate count among the highest in Europe.

## Munich Airport

Munich Airport (MUC) serves Munich and lies about 29 km from the city centre. Its 2 terminals hold 110 gates and it handles about 37 million passengers a year.
