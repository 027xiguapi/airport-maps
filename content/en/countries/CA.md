Canada is covered here by two airports: Toronto Pearson International (YYZ) and Vancouver International (YVR). Together they hold 4 terminals and 158 gates and handle about 75 million passengers a year, one gateway on each coast.

## Toronto Pearson International

Toronto Pearson International (YYZ) serves Toronto and lies about 22 km from the city centre. Its 2 terminals hold 100 gates, and at about 46 million passengers a year it is the largest Canadian airport covered here.

## Vancouver International

Vancouver International (YVR) serves Vancouver and lies about 12 km from the city centre. Its 2 terminals hold 58 gates and it handles about 29 million passengers a year, forming Canada's main gateway towards the Asia-Pacific.
