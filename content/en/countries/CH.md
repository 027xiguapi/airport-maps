Switzerland is covered here by a single airport: Zurich Airport (ZRH). It holds 4 terminals and 106 gates and handles about 31 million passengers a year — the largest Swiss airport in this directory.

## Zurich Airport

Zurich Airport (ZRH) serves Zurich and lies about 13 km from the city centre. Its 106 gates are spread across 4 terminals, a generous gate count for an airport of its size.
