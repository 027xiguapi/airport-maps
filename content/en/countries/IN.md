India is covered here by a single airport: Indira Gandhi International (DEL). It holds 2 terminals and 62 gates and handles about 73 million passengers a year — the largest airport covered in South Asia on this site.

## Indira Gandhi International

Indira Gandhi International (DEL) serves Delhi and lies about 16 km from the city centre. Its 62 gates are spread across 2 terminals, carrying a remarkably large flow for a two-terminal airport.

The airport page carries a terminal layout diagram, gate ranges, main airlines and ground transport options; search the directory by the IATA code DEL to jump straight to it.
