Mexico is covered here by a single airport: Mexico City International (MEX). It holds 2 terminals and 60 gates and handles about 50 million passengers a year, the main northern-Latin-American hub in this directory.

## Mexico City International

Mexico City International (MEX) serves Mexico City and lies only about 6 km from the city centre — one of the closest-to-town airports covered here. Its 60 gates are spread across 2 terminals.

The airport page carries a terminal layout diagram, gate ranges, main airlines and ground transport options; search the directory by the IATA code MEX to jump straight to it.
