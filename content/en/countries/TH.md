Thailand is covered here by a single airport: Suvarnabhumi (BKK). It holds 120 gates under one terminal roof and handles about 59 million passengers a year — the largest Thai airport in this directory.

## Suvarnabhumi

Suvarnabhumi (BKK) serves Bangkok and lies about 30 km from the city centre. Its 120 gates sit within one vast terminal — the second-largest single-terminal gate count covered here, behind Istanbul.
