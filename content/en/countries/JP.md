Japan is covered here by three airports: Tokyo Haneda (HND), Narita (NRT) and Kansai (KIX). Together they hold 8 terminals and 205 gates and handle about 149 million passengers a year, making this one of the most completely covered destinations in East Asia on this site.

## Tokyo's two airports

Haneda (HND) and Narita (NRT) are both listed under Tokyo, with different roles. Haneda sits about 14 km from central Tokyo — the closest of the three — with 3 terminals, 71 gates and roughly 88 million passengers a year. Narita lies about 66 km east of the city, and its 3 terminals hold 96 gates, the most of any Japanese airport covered here, for about 40 million passengers a year.

## Kansai International

Kansai (KIX) is about 50 km south-west of central Osaka, with 2 terminals and 38 gates handling roughly 21 million passengers a year — the only Osaka-area airport in this directory.
